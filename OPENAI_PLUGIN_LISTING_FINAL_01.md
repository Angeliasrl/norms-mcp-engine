## Primary listing

**Plugin name:** NORMS: Evidence & Scope

**Short description:** Assess structured, ratified normative records against verified grounds. See blockers, unknowns and unexamined areas; not legal advice.

**Long description:**

An empty blocker list does not prove that a normative basis has been verified. NORMS assesses normative records and facts that are already structured and ratified for a declared purpose. It checks explicit authority, temporal applicability, scope, verification, provision segmentation and structured conditions. It returns admissibility, eligibility, any limited authorization supported by the selected purpose, and explicit `blocking`, `unknown` and `unexamined` results.

NORMS does not create, retrieve or freely interpret laws; parse PDFs; approve contracts; independently verify user-supplied facts; provide legal advice; or certify overall conformity. Missing or unverified required grounds fail closed.

**Publisher:** Francesco Riva

**Conceptual category:** developer infrastructure / structured assessment. Select the final category only from the taxonomy displayed by the portal.

## Prudent alternatives

### Alternative A

**Short description:** Deterministic assessment for structured normative records, exposing blockers and unknowns without inferring law from free text.

**Long description:** NORMS evaluates whether already-structured, ratified normative records have sufficient explicit grounds for a declared purpose. It checks authority, scope, temporal applicability, verification, segmentation and structured conditions, then reports the limited assessment together with blockers, unknowns and unexamined areas. It is read-only, fails closed and does not retrieve or interpret laws, parse documents, verify facts independently, provide legal advice or certify overall conformity.

### Alternative B

**Short description:** Check verified grounds for structured normative records with deterministic, fail-closed results; not legal advice.

**Long description:** NORMS helps inspect the evidence boundary around a structured normative assessment. Inputs must already be structured and ratified. The tool evaluates only declared authority, scope, dates, verification, provision segmentation and conditions. Outputs distinguish blocking, unknown and unexamined areas from limited authorization. NORMS does not turn prose into law, search legal sources, accept PDFs, approve contracts, verify supplied facts independently or replace professional review.
